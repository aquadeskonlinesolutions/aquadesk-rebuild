Sound familiar?

Every dive center faces the same problems

📋
Paper registration forms
Divers fill out forms at the front desk. Handwriting is illegible. Forms get lost. Information has to be re-entered manually.
🧮
Manual billing calculations
Secretaries add up dive rates, taxes, and surcharges by hand. Errors happen. Disputes follow. Time is wasted.
🪣
Whiteboard scheduling
Tomorrow's dive plan is written on a board. Boats, divemasters, tank counts — all done manually every single morning.
📁
No diver history
A returning diver fills out the same form again. There's no record of their previous visits, certifications, or preferences.
💸
Lost revenue
No system means no reporting. Owners have no idea how much money was made today, this week, or this month without counting receipts.
😤
Overwhelmed secretaries
Front desk staff are buried in paperwork instead of helping divers. Peak hours are chaotic. Mistakes are inevitable.
Everything you need

One system. Every operation.

AquaDesk replaces every manual process in your dive center with a clean, fast, and reliable digital system.

📱
Digital diver registration
Send divers a registration link before they arrive. They fill it out on their phone — personal info, certifications, emergency contacts, and digital waiver signature.
📋
Smart diver form
Secretary searches a diver, loads their record, adds activity rows, and the bill calculates automatically. Supports PHP, USD, and EUR with custom exchange rates.
🖨️
Invoice printing
Generate a clean, formatted invoice for any diver in one click. Shows all activity line items, payment breakdown, and totals. Password-protected once paid.
🚤
Dive scheduling board
Plan tomorrow's dives on a digital board. Assign divers to boats and divemasters, calculate air and nitrox tanks, and save — diver forms update automatically.
⚡
Bulk activity updates
When a boat returns, select it and mark all divers as completed at once. No more searching each diver individually after every dive.
📊
Owner dashboard
Real-time revenue today, this week, and this month. Active divers, boats out, pending bills, low tank alerts — everything at a glance from any device.
👥
Group bookings
Send one registration link to a group of 40 divers. Track who has registered, who hasn't, and schedule the entire group in bulk.
💼
Staff & payroll
Track divemaster commissions automatically. Manage salary, daily rate, and per-dive commission. Generate payroll summaries every two weeks.
🏪
Equipment & tank inventory
Track BCDs, wetsuits, and all rental equipment. Monitor air and nitrox tank counts with low-stock alerts before operations begin.
